## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are developers, researchers, and contributors running OpenFilingRAG locally to ingest filings, inspect retrieved evidence, and validate source-grounded answers. They need a workbench that exposes document state and RAG execution clearly without feeling like a generic chat toy.

### Brand Personality
Calm, utilitarian, and transparent. The interface should feel like a serious local research tool: clear over flashy, dense over decorative, and operationally legible at every step.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light-mode-first research workbench with restrained neutrals, a small set of functional accents, and assistant-ui as the central interaction surface. Avoid dark neon, gradient-heavy AI styling, nested card piles, and decorative chrome.

### Design Principles
1. Keep file/document state visible and actionable.
2. Show retrieval and validation steps as structured workflow artifacts, not hidden reasoning.
3. Reserve visual emphasis for evidence quality, warnings, and active scope.
4. Prefer stable modules and explicit contracts over clever client-side orchestration.
5. Make the local app feel inspectable and contributor-friendly.
